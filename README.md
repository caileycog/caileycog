## Selected work

**[Psoriasis Biomarker Discovery with Interpretable Machine Learning](https://github.com/caileycog/psoriasis-biomarker-discovery)** · *MSc dissertation*
I combined differential expression analysis with a nested cross-validated comparison of XGBoost, Random Forest and SVM classifiers, then used SHAP to explain each model's decisions. This narrowed 21,510 genes down to **14 candidate biomarkers**, including six not previously linked to psoriasis.
`R` `limma` `XGBoost` `Random Forest` `SVM` `SHAP` `nested cross-validation`

**[Big Data Analysis of ICLR Submissions](https://github.com/caileycog/iclr-submissions-big-data-analysis)**
This is a distributed analysis of 55,906 submissions to a leading machine-learning conference, written to answer the programme committee's questions about its review process. A cross-validated random forest predicts acceptance from reviewer scores with an **AUC of 0.962**. I validated the pipeline against reference outputs from two earlier releases of the dataset.
`PySpark` `Spark SQL` `Spark MLlib` `Databricks`

**[Predicting Airline Passenger Satisfaction](https://github.com/caileycog/airline-passenger-satisfaction)**
This is a benchmark of interpretable and high-performance classifiers, with leakage-safe preprocessing, 5-fold cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning. Gradient boosted trees reached a **test AUC of 0.97**, and the logistic regression's odds ratios showed what drives satisfaction most, led by travel purpose and loyalty-scheme membership.
`KNIME` `logistic regression` `gradient boosted trees` `cross-validation`

**[Demographic Drivers of the 2016 EU Referendum Vote](https://github.com/caileycog/eu-referendum-voting-demographics)**
This analysis of the British Social Attitudes Survey uses chi-square tests, multicollinearity diagnostics and binary logistic regression. Educational attainment was the strongest predictor: voters with no qualifications had **5.8 times the odds** of voting Leave compared with graduates.
`IBM SPSS` `chi-square tests` `logistic regression` `survey data`

**[Metadata and Song Popularity in the Streaming Era](https://github.com/caileycog/song-popularity-metadata-visualisation)**
This is a set of five accessible visualisations of 1,377 Billboard Hot 100 songs. I chose each chart type based on research into how people read charts, and designed them for colour-blind and low-vision readers.
`R` `ggplot2` `tidyverse` `data visualisation`
